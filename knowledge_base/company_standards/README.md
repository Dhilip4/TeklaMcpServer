# Company Standards Knowledge

Place your company-specific detailing rules, fabrication guidelines, and naming conventions here.

## What Belongs Here
- Naming conventions for parts, assemblies, phases
- Standard edge distances and bolt patterns
- Preferred profile sizes and material grades
- Drawing title block standards
- Revision management rules
- Paint/finish specifications

## File Naming Convention
- `naming_conventions.md` — Part naming rules (COLUMN, RAFTER, BRACE, etc.)
- `bolt_standards.md` — Preferred bolt sizes, grades, and standards
- `drawing_rules.md` — Title block, scale, and layout requirements
- `fabrication_notes.md` — Standard fabrication notes and tolerances
